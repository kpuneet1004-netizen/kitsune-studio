03 / AGREEMENTREVIEW & ACCEPTANCE

Please review the complete agreement and project schedule before accepting. The signed version will identify the contracting parties, final scope, fees, payment dates, responsibilities, intellectual property terms, confidentiality, liability, termination, and governing law.

Scope and changes

Only deliverables listed in the approved project schedule are included. Additional pages, integrations, or functionality require a written change request and revised quote before work begins.

Payment and ownership

Payments follow the 50% / 20% / 30% milestones. Bespoke deliverable ownership transfers only after full payment, subject to the final signed agreement and third-party licences.

Delays and closure

Client feedback and materials are required to maintain the schedule. After 30 consecutive days of inactivity, written notice provides a further 7 days to resume before the project may be closed under the agreement.

Legal review

The final agreement must be completed with the correct legal identity of the service provider and reviewed for applicable Indian law and stamp duty before execution.
