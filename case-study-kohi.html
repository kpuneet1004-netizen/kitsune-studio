01 / THE INTENT

Make the mood immediate.

The first screen should feel like the café itself: welcoming, unhurried, and unmistakably warm. An oversized serif headline and a simple coffee-cup illustration establish the atmosphere without making visitors wait for the point.

02 / THE STRUCTURE

A simple path to a visit.

The page moves from feeling to practical information: a short story, a focused menu, a brand point of view, then a clear visit prompt. The mobile layout stacks content into easy-to-scan sections with a compact navigation menu.

03 / THE VISUAL SYSTEM

Warmth without clutter.

Oat, espresso, terracotta, and muted green create a hospitality palette. Editorial type, fine dividers, and restrained card layouts give the design character while keeping the menu and calls to action legible.
