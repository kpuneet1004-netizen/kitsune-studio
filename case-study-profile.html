01 / THE PROFILE

Make the professional story feel human and credible.

The supplied website presents Prashant Bhandari as a customer-success professional focused on customer engagement, retention, account ownership, and revenue growth. The experience uses a confident editorial voice while keeping career details and contact routes easy to find.

POSITIONING

People first. Results always.

A memorable statement connects relationship-building with the commercial outcomes behind the work.

PRIMARY AUDIENCE

Recruiters and hiring teams

Visitors can quickly assess role fit, performance indicators, professional experience, and how to start a conversation.

CORE PROMISE

Proof before buzzwords

Metrics and career context give the headline substance instead of relying on generic resume language.

02 / THE BRIEF

Turn a resume into a clear personal destination.

The supplied production-polished website was used as the design reference. The goals below describe the delivered interface and its apparent intent; they are not presented as user-research findings.

Design goals

  • Communicate professional positioning within the opening screen.
  • Put measurable business impact high in the information hierarchy.
  • Make career history, education, and contact options straightforward to explore.
  • Build a distinctive visual identity without sacrificing resume readability.
  • Provide direct access to LinkedIn and a downloadable resume.

Experience requirements

  • Clear navigation across intro, impact, about, experience, education, and contact.
  • Responsive layout with usable touch targets.
  • Metrics that are easy to scan and understand.
  • Accessible semantic content, keyboard navigation, and visible focus states.
  • Functional mail and resume links.
03 / THE CHALLENGE

Balance a strong first impression with substantive proof.

A conventional CV often makes readers work to connect responsibilities with outcomes. This concept reframes the resume as a guided narrative: positioning first, measurable impact next, then the context and experience behind those numbers.

CHALLENGE 01

Earn attention quickly

A concise headline and portrait-led composition introduce the person before the detailed career timeline.

CHALLENGE 02

Make impact scannable

Large numeric metrics create a quick summary of revenue, engagement, account ownership, and recognition.

CHALLENGE 03

Make the next step easy

LinkedIn and email contact options reduce the distance between reviewing the profile and reaching out.

04 / DESIGN DECISIONS

A guided narrative, from identity to evidence.

The page structure is designed to answer a reader’s questions in sequence: who is this person, what impact have they had, how do they work, and where can I learn more?

01 / POSITIONING FIRST

Lead with a clear point of view

“People first. Results always.” is short enough to remember and specific enough to frame the rest of the portfolio.

02 / EVIDENCE SECOND

Turn outcomes into visual anchors

The impact section uses high-contrast figures and concise explanations to make the key claims easy to scan.

03 / CONTEXT THIRD

Give the numbers a story

About, experience, education, and contact sections provide the context behind the headline and metrics.

05 / DESIGN SYSTEM

Warm editorial typography, measured structure.

The supplied site combines a warm off-white canvas with dark ink, a portrait-led composition, serif display type, and compact mono labels. This creates a professional portfolio that feels personal rather than like a generic CV template.

06 / RESPONSIVE UX

Keep the story clear on every screen.

Small-screen priorities

  • Headline, primary actions, and key profile information remain prominent.
  • Multi-column layouts collapse into a comfortable vertical flow.
  • Metric cards reflow without forcing horizontal page scrolling.
  • Buttons and links remain easy to tap.
  • Text scales with the viewport while preserving a clear hierarchy.

Accessibility & resilience

  • Semantic headings and page landmarks.
  • Keyboard navigation and visible focus styles.
  • Descriptive alt text for portrait imagery.
  • Reduced-motion support in the supplied stylesheet.
  • Direct contact and PDF resume links.

08 / HANDOFF & LIMITATIONS

A polished story, with clear boundaries.

Included in the supplied website

  • Personal introduction and profile positioning.
  • Impact metrics, professional experience, and education sections.
  • Responsive layouts and navigation interactions.
  • LinkedIn, email, and downloadable resume pathways.

Before a public launch

  • Confirm that all metrics, dates, and role descriptions are approved for public use.
  • Check the resume PDF and email destination on desktop and mobile.
  • Run keyboard, screen-reader, and real-device browser checks.
  • Review analytics and privacy requirements before adding tracking.
