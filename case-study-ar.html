The challenge
in plain language.

An architecture portfolio has two jobs: show the work and reveal the thinking behind it. The door-led entrance creates an emotional first impression, while project metadata, design philosophy, and process make Elena’s multidisciplinary practice easier to understand.

This fictional concept tests a quiet-luxury visual direction with an animated doorway, warm material palette, clear project categories, and a mobile-first reading experience.

The design
approach.

Let atmosphere set the tone. Instrument Serif headlines, bronze details, dark timber, and warm light create an architectural editorial identity.

Keep navigation clear. Work, About, and Notes are simple labels that support scanning rather than compete with it.

Let the work lead. Large architectural images, asymmetrical project cards, and short metadata captions keep the attention on each project.

01 / IDENTITY

Atmospheric first impression

A centered double-door entrance, warm bronze accents, and editorial typography make the portfolio feel like entering a real space.

02 / DISCOVERY

Work first, friction low

Project-led navigation helps a visitor move quickly from the introduction to relevant examples.

03 / RESPONSIVE

Designed for every screen

The door scales to phone screens, project cards collapse to one column, and the entrance respects reduced-motion settings.
