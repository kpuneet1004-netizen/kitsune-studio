02 / A design philosophy

Form follows
feeling.

“A space becomes meaningful when it feels inevitable — when light, material, and purpose find their balance.”

Elena Rossi is a fictional multidisciplinary architect and spatial designer created by Kitsune Studio to demonstrate a complete personal-brand portfolio. Her imagined practice moves between architecture, interiors, and spatial concepts, with a consistent interest in natural materials, quiet geometry, and the everyday rituals that make a place feel like home.

This concept is built around a simple belief: thoughtful design should be felt before it is explained.

01 / LIGHT

Make room for light.

Daylight is treated as a material in its own right — shaping rhythm, atmosphere, and how a space is experienced.

02 / MATERIAL

Let materials speak.

Stone, timber, plaster, and metal are selected for their character, tactility, and ability to age with grace.

03 / HUMAN

Design for living.

Every proportion and transition begins with the people who will move through, gather in, and return to a space.

03 / The approach

From first thought
to felt experience.

A considered process makes space for context, curiosity, and craft. The outcome is not a style applied to every brief, but a coherent response to each place and purpose.

01 / Observe

Understand the place.

Read the site, its light, its material conditions, and the people who will inhabit it.

02 / Imagine

Find the essential idea.

Explore proportions, movement, and atmosphere before settling on a visual language.

03 / Refine

Give detail its purpose.

Bring materials, construction logic, and spatial experience into a thoughtful whole.

04 / Realise

Make the idea tangible.

Carry the intent through drawings, collaboration, and the decisions that shape the final experience.
