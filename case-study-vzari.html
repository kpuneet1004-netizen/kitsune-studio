A familiar category.
A more considered journey.

VZARI helps people in Bengaluru enquire about selling pre-owned silk and zari sarees. The website needed to do more than display beautiful textiles: it had to explain what the business considers, set expectations around evaluation, and help visitors take the next step with confidence.

The experience brings together a clear service proposition, textile-led visual storytelling, practical guidance and a direct WhatsApp handoff. The language is deliberately careful: an enquiry begins an individual review, while any visit, valuation or purchase is confirmed separately.

01 / POSITIONING

Make the offer clear

Explain who VZARI serves, the kinds of textiles considered and the Bengaluru service area from the first screen.

02 / TRUST

Set useful expectations

Describe the review process without promising a guaranteed valuation, offer or purchase.

03 / ACTION

Reduce enquiry friction

Make WhatsApp the direct next step, with prompts for locality, quantity and photographs.

Designed around
the textile and its story.

A warm paper palette, deep wine accents and restrained gold details create a visual language that feels connected to traditional silk without becoming ornamental or difficult to read. Editorial serif typography gives the brand room to express heritage, while a clean sans-serif system keeps navigation and service information practical.

Photography does the heavy lifting. Product imagery gives visitors a more tangible sense of the materials and categories VZARI considers, while generous spacing and short content blocks keep the experience calm and scannable.

02 / EXPERIENCE DESIGN

From discovery
to a useful first enquiry.

The site is organised to answer the visitor’s most immediate questions before asking them to get in touch.

STEP 01

Understand

Know what VZARI does, where it operates and what kinds of textiles it considers.

STEP 02

Explore

Browse relevant saree categories, service details and educational guidance.

STEP 03

Feel informed

Review the process, FAQs and practical expectations before making contact.

STEP 04

Enquire

Move into WhatsApp with a clear prompt to share locality, quantity and photos.

Built as a connected
multi-page system.

The project extends beyond the homepage into dedicated service and category pages, Bengaluru locality pages, a how-to-sell guide, FAQs, contact information and educational journal content. Shared navigation, page patterns and enquiry calls-to-action help visitors move through the site without losing the main purpose.

The implementation uses semantic HTML, custom responsive CSS and lightweight vanilla JavaScript, with static pages served through Cloudflare Workers Static Assets. The enquiry journey hands off to WhatsApp; visitors review and send the pre-filled message themselves.
