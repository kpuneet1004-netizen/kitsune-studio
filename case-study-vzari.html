01 · The challenge & audience

Visitors may arrive with a single old saree or a family collection, often unsure whether the pieces are suitable or what the evaluation involves. The website needed to explain the service quickly, answer practical questions and make it easy to begin without implying that every enquiry leads to a purchase.

02 · Strategy & visual direction

The visual direction uses textile photography as the primary storytelling device, supported by warm paper surfaces and restrained heritage-inspired accents. The delivered website pairs warm paper (#F7F3E9), deep ink (#26231F), wine (#552B2C), antique gold (#B58A32) and muted taupe. Playfair Display brings an editorial heritage quality while DM Sans keeps evaluation and enquiry information direct and legible.

03 · Information architecture & interaction

The experience moves from the core service proposition into the saree types considered, the evaluation process, customer feedback and FAQs. Dedicated service, how-to-sell, FAQ and blog pages support deeper discovery. Repeated WhatsApp calls-to-action keep the enquiry route visible, with a pre-filled message that visitors review and send themselves.

04 · Responsive build & delivery

The site is built with semantic HTML, custom responsive CSS and lightweight vanilla JavaScript, and deployed on Cloudflare. Shared page patterns support a consistent multi-page experience, while responsive image variants, lazy loading and restrained scroll reveals keep the interface practical across devices. The WhatsApp handoff does not transmit enquiry details to a VZARI website database.
