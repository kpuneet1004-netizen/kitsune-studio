03 / BUSINESS WEBSITE · VZARI

Heritage, made
easier to discover.

A digital experience for VZARI, a Bengaluru-based business reviewing pre-owned silk, zari and heirloom sarees. The brief called for a clear, trustworthy presence that respects the value and story behind every piece — while making the first enquiry feel simple.

Business website28 canonical pagesLocal SEO architectureWhatsApp enquiry flow
01 / THE CHALLENGE

Make a considered service feel clear and approachable.

Selling a treasured saree can be personal. The website needed to explain what VZARI considers, what information helps with an initial review, and what happens next — without making an enquiry feel like a commitment.

01 / TRUST

Explain the process

Set expectations around individual review, transparent offers and the customer's freedom to decide.

02 / DISCOVERY

Build useful pathways

Organise saree categories, service information and Bengaluru locality pages around real search intent.

03 / ACTION

Reduce enquiry friction

Make WhatsApp the clear next step, with prompts for photographs, saree details and locality.

02 / THE DESIGN

A visual language inspired by craft and material.

Warm, tactile, editorial

A warm paper foundation, deep wine accents and restrained antique-gold details give the interface a heritage sensibility without leaning on ornamental clutter. Textile photography carries the visual weight.

Designed to feel straightforward

  • Responsive layouts with clear reading hierarchy
  • Category-led textile imagery and descriptive content
  • Consistent navigation and page-level calls to action
  • Accessible form controls and reduced-motion support
03 / THE BUILD

One coherent system. Many useful entry points.

CONTENT ARCHITECTURE

28 canonical pages

Homepage, service and saree guides, Bengaluru locality pages, FAQs, contact, about and educational journal content.

ENGINEERING

Lean by design

Semantic HTML, custom CSS and vanilla JavaScript, served as static assets through Cloudflare Workers.

CONVERSION

WhatsApp-first journey

Enquiry details are validated in the browser and handed off in a pre-filled WhatsApp message. Visitors choose when to send.

The page architecture and implementation describe the delivered website. Search rankings, enquiry volume and commercial outcomes are not claimed here.
